# 2017 Infiniti Q50 3.0t: diagnostic findings, 2026-10-17

Vehicle: VIN JN1SAMPLEXXXXXXXX (placeholder; the real VIN read with OBD mode 09 is withheld from this copy, and the placeholder fails the VIN check digit). No DAS: Drive Mode Selector offers Standard/Sport steering only and there is no PS fluid reservoir, so the car has a rack electric power steering (EPS) unit. Complaint: steering intermittently feels stiff and sluggish.
Adapter: vLinker FS r2, STN1170 v4.3.2, 115200 baud, about 16 ms per command.

## Summary

- The EPS module stores no trouble codes. During the parked wheel-turn test, supply voltage stayed above 13.2 V, assist rose with steering effort, and the assist-limit candidate stayed at 100 in every sample. No captured value shows an EPS fault. The complaint is intermittent and was not reproduced during a capture with EPS data.
- Three other modules store confirmed (status 0x08), not currently failing, codes: RADAR C1A01-00, RADAR C1A16-00, AIR BAG B00A0-00. Their Q50 meanings need the factory service manual.
- The engine is clean: MIL off, 0 OBD codes, all readiness monitors complete.
- The OBD port is gatewayed. An unfiltered 5 s bus monitor received 0 broadcast frames, so steering data must be polled from the modules.

## EPS module

- Identification (21 83): `5CA0A.B.......301A..10A.`. Local ID 21 84 reads `5320160901`. Inference: a 2016-09-01 production date.
- Answers Nissan local IDs 21 xx only. Six 22 xxxx ranges returned nothing. 21 06 is a 280-byte block; inference: a stored event history, not decoded.
- Live block 21 01 (21 bytes), decoded from 97 samples with the wheel at rest and 215 samples during lock-to-lock turns, compared with the ABS steering angle:

| Bytes | Meaning | Confidence | Observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-1 | Supply voltage, x 4 mV | Matches ATRV | 14.0-14.25 V at rest, 13.26 V pushing into full lock |
| 3-4 | Steering angle, signed x -0.1 deg | Correlation -1.00 with ABS angle | -543 to +553 deg |
| 5-6 | Steering velocity, signed | Inferred | About -3.7 deg/s per count, 0 when held |
| 7 | Assist load | Inferred | 0 at rest, up to 25 at full lock |
| 8 | Driver torque, signed | Inferred | +49 holding left lock, -59 pushing right lock |
| 10 | Temperature candidate | Inferred | Rose from 45 to 53 as the car warmed |
| 11 | Assist limit candidate | Inferred | 100 in every sample |

## Drive recording (2026-10-17, not included in this repository)

330 s, 3 "Steering felt heavy" marks: at 5 s (stopped, -15 deg), 31 s (2 km/h, +533 deg, near full lock), and 57 s (36 km/h, -8 deg). Voltage at all marks was 14.0-14.3 V, so low voltage is excluded as the cause of those events. The recording has no EPS data, and speed/angle were sampled only every 3.7 s, so assist at the marks is unknown.

## Background research (docs/research/q50-diagnostics.md)

No recall or TSB was found for non-DAS Q50 EPS. NHTSA complaints 11174708 and 11197207 describe similar symptoms. Generic Nissan EPS codes (C1601-C1613, U1000) are the ones to watch; none are stored.

## Next steps

1. Drive with the rebuilt Steering page recording and mark each heavy event. The new recording includes EPS assist, torque and assist limit. Its sample rate on the car is not yet measured.
2. Look up C1A01, C1A16 and B00A0 in the Q50 service manual.
3. If a marked event shows normal assist load and no limit reduction, the cause is likely outside the EPS electronics. Candidates such as tire pressure, alignment, tie rods or rack friction are inferences; none of them were measured today.
