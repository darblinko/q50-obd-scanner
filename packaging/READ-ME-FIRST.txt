Q50 DIAG: READ ME FIRST
=======================

You do not need to install anything else. Everything the app needs is in
the zip file you downloaded.

What you need
- A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC (64-bit), or a Mac with macOS 14 (Sonoma)
  or newer. Older Macs cannot run the app.
- About 1.5 GB of free disk space.
- The USB car adapter (vLinker FS or OBDLink EX). Without it you can still
  try the app with the built-in simulator.


WINDOWS
-------
1. Right-click the downloaded file "q50diag-windows-x64.zip" and choose
   "Extract All...", then click "Extract". Do not start the app from inside
   the zip file.
2. Open the extracted "Q50 Diag" folder and double-click "q50diag" (its type
   is "Application").
3. A blue box saying "Windows protected your PC" may appear. Click
   "More info", then click "Run anyway". This box appears because the app is
   not signed with a paid certificate. You only do this once.
4. If a message says "Smart App Control blocked" the app, there is no button
   to allow it. Stop and contact the person who sent you the app.

Keep "q50diag" inside the "Q50 Diag" folder, next to the "_internal"
folder. It does not work on its own. To start it from the desktop, right-click
"q50diag" and choose "Send to", then "Desktop (create shortcut)". On
Windows 11, choose "Show more options" first.

The first start takes a few seconds longer than later starts.


MAC
---
1. Pick the right download. Click the Apple menu, then "About This Mac":
   - If it says "Chip: Apple M1" (or M2, M3, M4 and so on), use
     q50diag-macos-arm64.zip
   - If it says "Processor: Intel", use q50diag-macos-intel.zip
2. Double-click the zip file. A "Q50 Diag" folder appears. Drag
   "Q50 Diag" (the app) from that folder into your Applications folder.
3. Open Applications and double-click "Q50 Diag". The Mac says it cannot
   verify the app. Click "Done". Do NOT click "Move to Trash".
4. Open System Settings, then "Privacy & Security". Scroll down to the
   "Security" section. Next to the line about "Q50 Diag", click
   "Open Anyway". Type your Mac password, then click "Open Anyway" once more.
   Do this within an hour of step 3, or repeat step 3 first.
5. From now on, open the app with a normal double-click.

If the Mac says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", contact the person
who sent you the app.


CONNECTING TO THE CAR
---------------------
1. Plug the adapter into the car's diagnostic port (under the dashboard on
   the driver's side). Plug its USB cable into the computer. Turn the
   ignition on.
2. Windows only: the first time, wait about a minute while Windows sets up
   the adapter by itself. The PC must be connected to the internet.
3. In the app, open the "Connect" page. The adapter is listed at the top,
   usually marked "likely vLinker". On Windows its name looks like COM3; on
   a Mac it looks like /dev/cu.usbserial-something. Select it and click
   "Connect".
4. No car nearby? Select "SIMULATOR" in the same list and click "Connect".


MECHANIQ, THE AI ASSISTANT (RIGHT-HAND PANEL)
---------------------------------------------
MechanIQ answers questions about your car and the app. It needs an Anthropic
API key. Everything else in the app works without one.

1. Get a key: sign in at platform.claude.com/settings/keys (a paid
   account) and click "Create Key". Copy the key.
2. In the app, open MechanIQ settings, paste your API key and save.
3. Keep the key private, like a password. Anyone who has it can use your
   paid account.

On a small screen, click ">>" at the top of the MechanIQ panel to fold it
away and give the page more room. Pages that still do not fit get scroll
bars.


WHERE YOUR FILES ARE
--------------------
Recordings (.csv files) and the app log:
- Windows: open a File Explorer window, click in the address bar, paste
      %LOCALAPPDATA%\q50diag\app\logs
  and press Enter.
- Mac: in Finder, open the "Go" menu, choose "Go to Folder...", paste
      ~/Library/Application Support/q50diag/app/logs
  and press Return.

App settings and your MechanIQ API key are kept in a folder named
".config\q50diag" (Mac: ".config/q50diag") inside your user folder.


UPDATING
--------
Download the new zip. On Windows, replace the old "Q50 Diag" folder with the
new one. On a Mac, replace the old "Q50 Diag" app in Applications. Your
recordings, settings and API key are kept. Changes that MechanIQ made to the
app itself are not carried over; a copy of them is kept in a folder whose
name starts with "app-backup", next to the "app" folder named above.
